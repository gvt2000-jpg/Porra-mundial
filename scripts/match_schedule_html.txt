<h2 id="Match_schedule">Match schedule</h2></div>
<p>The match schedule, without group assignments, was announced on February 4, 2024.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Schedule&#95;3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Schedule-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-:6&#95;167-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-167"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>150<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Q&amp;A&#95;168-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q&amp;A-168"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>151<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On June 13, 2024, FIFA released an updated schedule, with specific pairings assigned to venues for the knockout stage.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-169" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-169"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>152<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In addition, group stage matches were assigned to specific groups (though pairings for non-host groups were not assigned to specific matches until after the final draw; thus the group venues were known, but not for which specific pairing each matchday). The full schedule was unveiled in a live broadcast on December 6, 2025, the day after the draw. Group stage pairings were allocated to specific matches, and the kickoff times were confirmed for all fixtures.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-170" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-170"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>153<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The opening match was announced to include Mexico, taking place on June 11, 2026, at the <a href="/wiki/Estadio_Azteca" title="Estadio Azteca">Estadio Azteca</a> in <a href="/wiki/Mexico_City" title="Mexico City">Mexico City</a>. This match will include South Africa.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-171" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-171"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>154<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The opening match involving Canada will take place on June 12 at <a href="/wiki/BMO_Field" title="BMO Field">BMO Field</a> in <a href="/wiki/Toronto" title="Toronto">Toronto</a>, while the opening match for the United States will take place on the same day at <a href="/wiki/SoFi_Stadium" title="SoFi Stadium">SoFi Stadium</a> in <a href="/wiki/Inglewood,_California" title="Inglewood, California">Inglewood</a>. Each host nation is scheduled to play its three matches in the group stage within its own country.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-:6&#95;167-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-167"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>150<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/AT%26T_Stadium" title="AT&amp;T Stadium">AT&amp;T Stadium</a> in <a href="/wiki/Arlington,_Texas" title="Arlington, Texas">Arlington, Texas</a>, will host the most matches of any venue at the tournament with nine. <a href="/wiki/MetLife_Stadium" title="MetLife Stadium">MetLife Stadium</a> in <a href="/wiki/East_Rutherford,_New_Jersey" title="East Rutherford, New Jersey">East Rutherford, New Jersey</a>, will host the <a href="/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_final" title="2026 FIFA World Cup final">final</a> on July 19. The United States will host 78 matches, including from the quarterfinal stage onward, while Canada and Mexico will each host 13. Each tournament venue, except for the <a href="/wiki/Estadio_Akron" title="Estadio Akron">Estadio Akron</a>, will host at least one knockout stage fixture.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-172" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-172"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>155<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The match schedule will overlap with the <a href="/wiki/2026_CFL_season" title="2026 CFL season">2026 CFL season</a>, resulting in scheduling conflicts and loss of home games for the <a href="/wiki/Toronto_Argonauts" title="Toronto Argonauts">Toronto Argonauts</a> and <a href="/wiki/BC_Lions" title="BC Lions">BC Lions</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-173" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-173"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>156<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-174" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-174"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>157<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The match schedule will also affect the schedules of the <a href="/wiki/Kansas_City_Royals" title="Kansas City Royals">Kansas City Royals</a>, <a href="/wiki/Philadelphia_Phillies" title="Philadelphia Phillies">Philadelphia Phillies</a>, <a href="/wiki/Seattle_Mariners" title="Seattle Mariners">Seattle Mariners</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Texas_Rangers_(baseball)" title="Texas Rangers (baseball)">Texas Rangers</a> of <a href="/wiki/Major_League_Baseball" title="Major League Baseball">Major League Baseball</a>, whose home stadiums are located near World Cup venues.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-175" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-175"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>158<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Host cities were geographically grouped into three regions:<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Schedule&#95;3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Schedule-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<ul><li>Western Region (Vancouver, Seattle, San Francisco, Los Angeles)</li>
<li>Central Region (Guadalajara, Mexico City, Monterrey, Houston, Dallas, Kansas City)</li>
<li>Eastern Region (Atlanta, Miami, Toronto, Boston, Philadelphia, New York/New Jersey)</li></ul>
